School controls & data safety

Designed for teacher-reviewed onboarding before any real children are enrolled.

Account and school administration

  • School, district and classroom membership
  • Teacher-managed invitations and student aliases
  • Guardian-to-student verification
  • Parental consent status and access history
  • Classroom enrollment and transfers
  • Account suspension, deletion and export workflows
  • Administrative audit events and least-privilege access

Database readiness

Database design includes separate students, users, schools, classrooms, guardians, reading events and quiz attempts—with row-level access rules.

Not ready for real student data: first connect the database provider, run migrations and automated access tests, configure guardian/school consent, verify authentication settings, and review district privacy requirements.
