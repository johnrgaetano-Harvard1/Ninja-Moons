How a race works

1. Choose a saved story—or paste an entirely new one. Stories and questions can be reused as often as you like.

2. Project the 30-lane leaderboard. Students enter the classroom code and a nickname on their laptops.

3. Students read the full passage and answer the comprehension challenges. Correct answers advance their silver ships.

4. The board moves each student's entire lane up or down as their ranking changes.

No real student accounts are required for this pilot. Use classroom nicknames; don't enter surnames or other private information.
