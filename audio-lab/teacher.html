How this test protects audio

Nothing is recorded or saved by the test app. The microphone runs only after the reader presses Start and grants permission. To stop, the reader taps Stop microphone; you can end any connection here. The app uses browser WebRTC with encrypted transport, but does not yet have school authentication or TURN fallback.
