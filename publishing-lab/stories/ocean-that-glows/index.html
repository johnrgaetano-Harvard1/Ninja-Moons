Arrival at Neruma

1 Kai stood at a round window as their ship neared Neruma, a moon wrapped in shifting blue oceans and thin cloud trails. 2 Maya leaned close, watching silver islands of foam sweep under sunlight. 3 Luma lit the floor with a pale map that shimmered like water. 4 Beyond the window, the horizon curved beneath a sky the color of a pearl. 5 The station came into view, a ring of white platforms surrounding a clear central pool. 6 Walkways connected round rooms with roofs like seashells. 7 Kai followed the map with one finger, tracing their route from the landing deck to the ocean below. 8 Their expedition was about to continue underwater.

A Clue to Investigate

18 “They said it passed over a reef and later the coral brightened,” Doctor Sol said. 19 She played a video: pale coral under a stone arch, a huge shape drifting through, silver threads falling like dust through light. 20 Later images showed that coral opening and warming with color while coral beyond the threads stayed pale. 21 Maya whispered, “What did the sailors actually see?” 22 “A pattern worth checking,” Doctor Sol replied. 23 “Not a cure for everything—evidence comes first.” 24 The recording showed several visits over many days. 25 Each image carried a date, so the explorers could compare the reef before and after the creature passed. 26 Doctor Sol explained that the ancient account had inspired a legend about healing. 27 Their new observation offered something specific to investigate. 28 Kai opened a notebook and drew two columns, one for what they noticed and another for questions.

The Coral Garden

29 They settled into a transparent submarine. 30 Water light poured through every wall. 31 Purple fans swayed beside orange coral towers. 32 Fish gathered and scattered like ribbon sparks. 33 Kai said, “Everything seems connected.” 34 Doctor Sol nodded. 35 “An ecosystem is living things plus surroundings. 36 Today we explore one.” 37 Even the floor beneath their shoes was clear. 38 Looking down felt like floating above a garden spread across the ocean bottom. 39 Sunlight made moving golden squares on their seats. 40 Farther below, rounded stones lay among pink branches and soft green ribbons. 41 The submarine drifted slowly enough for Maya to notice tiny striped fish peeking between the coral. 42 Through the curved roof, the surface sparkled like thousands of little mirrors.

Meet the Lumara

43 Near the three stones, the Lumara appeared, fins slow as curtains, blue spots glowing. 44 It looped over golden coral without breaking a branch. 45 Tiny golden specks drifted from coral toward its tail. 46 As they settled, its spots shone brighter. 47 Maya sketched the exchange and wrote, “What might the specks be doing?” 48 The creature was longer than their submarine, with a smooth silver back and fins edged in violet. 49 When it turned, each blue spot seemed to become a small star. 50 Its graceful movement stirred the golden branches, sending more specks upward. 51 Kai watched a single speck land beside one shining spot. 52 Luma saved the moment without adding an explanation.

Growing Possibilities

75 Over days, containers with golden material made more silvery substance than those without. 76 The same pattern appeared when they repeated it. 77 Maya said quietly, "Something huge might depend on something almost invisible." 78 Doctor Sol answered, "That’s why we ask more questions." 79 Their next project was a small growing chamber with clear walls and curved tubes. 80 They tried different settings and recorded each result. 81 Eventually, one setting produced a steady supply of the silvery material. 82 Doctor Sol called this biotechnology: using living things to make useful products. 83 The samples would stay in Neruma's laboratory while their findings traveled to Earth. 84 Scientists could investigate the compounds, repeat the tests, and explore whether any might help develop medicines. 85 Coral recovery alone could not establish that. 86 Kai pictured a discovery beginning with a notebook, a question, and someone willing to look closely. 87 Before their last evening ended, they visited the ocean once more. 88 Their submarine rested above the sandy clearing while the garden slowly filled with blue light. 89 The Lumara moved through the arch, passed the purple fans, and turned toward the golden branches again. 90 Maya checked the time beside her earlier notes. 91 Kai drew arrows showing the direction of the floating specks and silver threads. 92 On the ride back, he arranged their pictures across a table. 93 One showed the creature beside the coral. 94 Another showed the tiny shapes under the microscope. 95 A third showed the containers and their labels. 96 Each picture held part of the story, but none explained everything alone. 97 Maya opened a fresh page. 98 She wanted the next readers to compare those pictures, notice the repeated changes, and decide which clues mattered most. 99 Luma gathered the records into a single folder. 100 Outside the station, the ocean reflected a sky full of stars. 101 Somewhere beneath that bright surface, the Lumara continued its quiet journey through the branches of its extraordinary, beautifully glowing underwater garden.
