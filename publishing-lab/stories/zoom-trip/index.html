5 Words to Know

For teachers and grown-ups (not narrated)

Read the story aloud first; invite children to join in with “far, far, far,” “just seven,” and the countdown. Ask them to identify at least one clue before discussing the mystery.

Science note: Bending spacetime is an established concept in relativity, but the story’s ring making a quick shortcut is imaginary. No such space travel has been demonstrated. The panel converts sunlight into electricity for the pump. Mirrors can redirect sunlight, but the panel must collect and convert it.
